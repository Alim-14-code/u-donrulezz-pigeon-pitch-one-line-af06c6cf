One line, at most 200 characters: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup — an entry for make.fun's Pigeon Pitch, One Line bounty ($2, top 3 paid).

## Entry

> My startup delivers every crumb in the city in under ten minutes, by pigeon, no app required — I'm raising a seed round, and I mean actual seed.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
